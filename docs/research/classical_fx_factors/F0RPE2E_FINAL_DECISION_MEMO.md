## Decision

`BLOCKED_BY_PROSPECTIVE_INTEGRITY`

The original F0 rate-provenance block remains valid. The F0-RP amendment
remains frozen, and NZD/NZDUSD remain excluded. No NZD rate request or NZDUSD
market request was made.

endpoint. Official endpoint certification, storage budgeting, full acquisition,
and the infrastructure freeze were not reached. Development, validation, and
replication were not accessed, so their preregistered order remained intact.

## Prospective Stop

During official documentation discovery, an external search result snippet
incidentally exposed SONIA rows from 2025. The query did not request that date
range, and no adapter request, market request, source snapshot, clean-room row,
or result was persisted. Nevertheless, the absolute quarantine rule means the
required claim that 2023-2025 remained wholly unaccessed cannot be certified.
All further external access and empirical execution stopped immediately.

No performance metric was generated or replaced with zero. Development,
validation, replication, selection, and future prospective artifacts remain
`NOT_RUN` or `null`.

Any portfolio selected in a later uncontaminated gate would remain unconfirmed
until genuinely future prospective evidence satisfies the frozen requirements.
No result authorizes live-capital deployment.
