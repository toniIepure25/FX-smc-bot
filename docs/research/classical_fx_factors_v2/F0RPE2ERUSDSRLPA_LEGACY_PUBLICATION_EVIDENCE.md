# Gate F0-RP-E2E-R-USDSR-LPA Legacy Publication Evidence

Status: `PASS_DAY_LEVEL_PUBLICATION_RULE`.

The contemporaneous New York Fed operating-policy statements from February 2, 2015 and January 6, 2016 establish that the federal funds effective rate was published daily. The 2015 statement says the legacy rate used transactions from the previous day. The 2016 transition notice gives a concrete ordering example: the rate for March 1, 2016 was scheduled for publication on March 2.

New York Fed publication information also establishes that EFFR publication follows the New York Fed holiday schedule and that activity preceding a holiday is published on the subsequent business day. This supports a deterministic publication business day derived from the observation day and the frozen calendar.

The evidence does not establish an exact legacy publication clock instant. No approximate modern schedule is projected backward as an actual legacy timestamp. The certifiable relation is observation day D, next authorized New York Fed publication business day P, then safe use only at the first portfolio execution after P has ended.

No numerical rate row or provider payload was accessed for this evidence audit.
