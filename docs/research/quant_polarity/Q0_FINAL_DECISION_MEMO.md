# Gate Q.0 Final Decision

## Decision

`BLOCKED_BY_HOLDOUT_INTEGRITY`

Gate Q.0 stopped before development certification, signal execution, model
fitting, benchmark construction, or outcome evaluation. The authorized
development acquisition had 181 complete side-month partitions, four unresolved
partitions, and 295 unchecked partitions when all workers were stopped.

## Integrity event

A broad repository tool-file enumeration exposed the filename of a local
temporary market-data artifact containing a holdout year. No file content was
read, no holdout rows or counts were computed, and no holdout provider request
was sent. Nevertheless, Gate Q.0 requires both holdout storage enumeration and
holdout structure inspection to remain false. Those two flags are therefore true,
and the gate cannot continue or claim an untouched confirmatory holdout.

## Research interpretation

The direction-flip clue was outcome-informed in the previous lineage. No Q.0
development or replication outcome evidence was produced. No historical or
replication result is confirmed live trading alpha. No result authorizes capital
deployment.

The Strategy-Alpha lineage remained closed. Q.0 did not use its row ledgers or
candidate outcomes as labels, did not access replication outcomes, did not create
a paper/live handoff, and did not create a pull request.

## Recovery requirement

A future attempt requires a fresh independent gate with a newly established
holdout-integrity baseline and commands scoped so that holdout-year storage
cannot be enumerated. Existing local Q.0 market data must not be represented as a
certified or untouched dataset.
