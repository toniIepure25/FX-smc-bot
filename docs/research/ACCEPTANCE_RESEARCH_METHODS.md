# Acceptance Research Methods

Prospective: data-source provenance, pair-scoped USDJPY development freeze, C4 preregistration, C5 handoff amendment, and C5-B-R transport rules were frozen before their corresponding outcome adjudications.

Confirmatory: event definition, confirmation semantics, next-bar execution, 120-minute bid/ask executable outcomes, primary non-overlap policy, exact matching, covariate balance, paired permutation tests, day-cluster bootstrap, placebo checks, and development/validation separation were applied as frozen.

Exploratory: C4-B mechanism redesign was derived from development evidence and explicitly labeled before validation. C5-B-R dual-positive transport diagnostics were validation-informed and stopped before holdout.

Post-validation diagnostic: reconciliation overlays resolved artifact hash provenance without changing scientific values. Transport standardization used exact-cell stabilized covariate weighting over preregistered bins.

Operational remediation: provider parity and fallback were documented after data-quality failures, including prevention of zero-row certification.

Holdout preservation: the 2023-2025 holdout remained unopened and is not authorized for this closed Acceptance family.
